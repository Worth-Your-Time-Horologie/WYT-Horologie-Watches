Crafted with intent

Every surface on a Daybreak is finished by decision, not by default. Brushing, beveling and polishing are placed to catch light with purpose, and to hold up to a decade of wear.

01

Case finishing

Alternating brushed and polished bevels, cut by hand before final buffing, so each facet reads as a distinct plane of light.

02

Dial texture

A fine concentric brush is applied under raking light to control glare while keeping the surface legible in low light.

03

Hands

Diamond-cut facets on the hour and minute hands are angled to throw a single clean line of reflected light at rest.

04

Applied markers

Solid indices are set and pinned individually, each checked for height and angle before the dial is closed.

05

Crown

A coin-edged, screw-down crown is knurled for grip under a leather glove and sealed against 100 m of water resistance.

06

Bracelet

Solid links are drilled and finished before assembly, with a taper that keeps weight balanced on the wrist.

Engineered for time

A mechanical watch measures time by mainspring and escapement alone. WYT adds one system beneath that mechanism: an electronic winding assist that keeps the spring charged, and steps aside from everything else.

WYT-001

A large-format, single-barrel automatic architecture in the tradition of classic hand-wound calibers — an exposed balance and going train, sized to leave clear room beneath the mainspring for the winding-assist module.

ARCHITECTURE

Movement

A single-barrel automatic base, laid out to leave room beneath the mainspring for the winding-assist controller.

TRANSMISSION

Power

The rotor and gear train transmit motion to the mainspring exactly as in a conventional automatic caliber.

REGULATION

Escapement

Balance and escapement remain fully mechanical — the only part of the watch permitted to tell the time.

ASSIST SYSTEM

Winding

A battery, controller and small motor top up the mainspring when the watch sits unworn, then disengage.

CONSTRUCTION

Case

A screw-down caseback and crown seal the movement while keeping it accessible for regular servicing.

SEALING

Water resistance

Gaskets at the crown, caseback and crystal are rated and tested to 100 metres.

MATERIALS

Alloys

Stainless steel case construction, chosen first for durability under everyday wear.

PHILOSOPHY

Restraint

Every added system must earn its place. Complexity for its own sake is not a design goal here.

ABOUT WYT

Worth Your Time

WYT exists at the point where engineering, design and everyday usability meet. We build mechanical watches for people who want something considered, not just purchased.

We are not trying to out-complicate the manufactures that came before us. We are trying to make watches that work the way people actually live — worn daily, wound imperfectly, and still on time.

"Less, but better." — WYT DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
PHASE 01

Concept definition

The case, dial and winding-assist concept for Daybreak are defined and locked in a single master specification.

PHASE 02

Mechanical prototype

A proven mechanical platform is used to test case geometry, dial legibility and wearability, kept separate from the final movement.

PHASE 03

Movement architecture

The in-house caliber is engineered: barrel, gear train, escapement, winding system, and electronics laid out together.

PHASE 04

Engineering prototypes

Working prototypes are built and tested for accuracy, power reserve, water resistance and durability.

PHASE 05

Production

Daybreak enters production to the locked specification, ready to leave the manufacture.

DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Less, but better

PROPORTION

Case, dial and hands are sized against each other first — decoration is only ever added after the proportions hold.

FUNCTION

Every feature must justify its presence by what it does on the wrist, not by how it photographs.

MECHANICAL INTEGRITY

The escapement alone keeps time. Electronics assist the spring and are never permitted to touch the display.

LEGIBILITY

A centered dial and clear AM/PM indication mean the time is readable at a glance, in any light.

WEARABILITY

Built for a decade of daily wear, not a display case — proportioned, sealed and finished accordingly.

INDIVIDUALITY

Distinct without shouting. A Daybreak is recognized on close inspection, not from across the room.
